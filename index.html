Backstory

Born to the Roaming Roses, a nomadic Gridanian trade guild, Allice’s early years were filled with music, laughter, and the scent of caravan spice. Her parents — Summer Rose and Qrow Rose-Branwen — taught her to barter, to read the wind, and to respect the balance of the forest.


When the Garleans came, all of that was stolen. At twelve, she was taken to a research facility in the occupied lands of Ala Mhigo. There, she was one of many subjected to void infusion trials, forced to bond with a lesser voidsent as part of a secret Reaper program. Few survived.


She did — but not unchanged.


By twenty, hardened by years of servitude and covert missions, she escaped under the cover of chaos, her voidsent’s power turning against her captors. Her return to Bittermill brought only ruin; her home was nothing but ashes and silence.


For years she drifted through the shadows of Eorzea — a mercenary, hunter, and avenger — her scythe both weapon and curse.


Recently, she found refuge among the Scholars Rest Free Company, where for the first time, she fights not for orders or coin, but for companionship and cause.

Personality & Habits

  • Speech: Calm, measured, low-toned. Uses few words, but each chosen precisely.
  • Demeanor: Reserved in public, protective in private circles.
  • Rarely raises her voice unless angered.
  • Sleeps lightly and keeps weapons within reach.
  • Strong dislike for Garlean experimentation and magitek prisons.
  • Tends to linger at the edge of crowded rooms.
